How to use this guide

Below are the tutorial videos, practice audios, lyrics, scores, and chords for each song in the Filipino Mass on 29 November 2026. The songs are listed in the order they will be performed during the mass. Cue is provided for each song to guide the timing.


If "Video Unavailable" is shown, the video is only available in YouTube. Clicking the unavailable link will take you to the video. If further assistance is needed, you may seek assitance in our group chat.


Some hymns may not have their instrumental audio, sheets, or chords available.


You are welcome to contribute your knowledge by harmonising according to your vocal classification, provided you are familiar with the harmony parts.



Choir Quick Guide

This table shows when the choir and instrumentalists are cued to begin each song. Use these cues as a guide and stay flexible as the Mass unfolds, since timing may change with the flow of the liturgical celebration.

Mass Part Song Cue
Entrance N/A Start when procession begins. Fade when procession finishes, and altar servers and the priest reaches the altar finishing the entrance ceremony.
Gloria Papuri sa Diyos Begin immediately after Penitential Rite finishes. ("Sometimes a song within the Penitential Rite or just after "Lord, have mercy" part is provided before Gloria")

(This part may be skipped in some liturgical occasions depending on the season. See liturgical calendar for more information: Liturgical Calendar - Universalis)
Gospel Acclamation Alleluia Start after the 2nd Reading.
Offertory Mula Sa 'Yo Start when gifts are brought forward. (If chosen song is too short, repeat a verse and end with chorus. If the song is too long, it may be shortened ending with a chorus.)
Sanctus Santo Immediately after "Holy, Holy, Holy..." ("...kaya kasama ng mga anghel at ng mga banal...").
Memorial Acclamation Sa Krus Mo at Pagkabuhay Immediately after consecration.
Amen Great Amen (WYD) Immediately after "Through Him, With Him..." ("Sa pamamagitan ni Kristo, kasama Niya at sa Kanya...")

(If too slow and commentator/priest says "Amen" first, mass will proceed immediately, skip this song)
Lord's Prayer Ama Namin Priest introduces the Our Father.
Doxology Sapagkat Sa'yo ang Kaharian Immediately after "Deliver us, Lord..." (Note: This may be skipped if the attempt to sing is too slow, priest recites the Doxology)
Agnus Dei Kordero ng Diyos Immediately after the invitation to the "Sign of Peace..." ("Kapayapaan ng Panginoon ang laging sumainyo...")
Communion 1 Pananabik Begin once congregation starts moving for Communion. (Might even be shortened if the queue is very short. Communion 2 will be skipped.)
Communion 2 Awit ng Paghilom Only if Communion queue is still ongoing.
Recessional Ang Puso Ko'y Nagpupuri After final blessing and dismissal.

Important!
Watch if the priest looks at the choir or a member of the choir provides hand signals to other members for visual cues. Songs may be shortened to a fade-out, repeated, skipped, or started early depending on the flow of the Mass.

In some special occasions, additional songs may be added, or some songs may be skipped. The choir should stay flexible and follow the flow of the Mass.
